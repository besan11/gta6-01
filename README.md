An original open-world crime game for the [make.fun gta6-01 "Make GTA 6" bounty](https://make.fun/competitions/gta6-01). No Rockstar content: the city, the people, the cars and the story are all invented for this entry.

Dario Cruz is home in Porto Verde after three years away. Mara Solis has work for him, Kessler owns the streets, and the city remembers everything. Twelve missions across three days — from a quiet pickup at the Marina Cafe to a full casino-vault heist on the last night, with the Porto Verde PD, a police helicopter, and the whole city answering the alarm.

## What is in the game

- A living coastal city: drive and walk, traffic, pedestrians, a beach with umbrellas and boats, six named districts, golden-hour light.
- Wanted level 0–5 with a PD response panel (unit, officer, distance, status), police cruisers that ram, and the AIR-1 helicopter at four stars.
- Combat on foot and from a car: fists, Tide 9mm, Squall SMG, Breaker 12, with ammo that matters.
- Missions with a story: briefings with character portraits, stages, a next-mission preview, timed missions, chases, defences and escapes.
- REP that grows with every job, a three-station radio, a 300×210 minimap with a pulsing route, saving and continuing, synthesized WebAudio sound.

## How to play

Serve the game (`node serve.ts`) and open http://localhost:8080.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / Arrows | Move / drive |
| E | Enter / exit car |
| F | Punch |
| Mouse click | Shoot |
| 1–4 | Switch weapon |
| R | Change radio station |
| Q | Save |
| M | Mute |
| Shift | Run |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| C | Continue (title screen, when a save exists) |
| T | Back to title |

Finish all 12 missions to win. Get to 0 HP and you are WASTED; get caught on foot at high heat and you are BUSTED.
